# eng-curve-digitization/ — digitized moment-arm bands (Blemker + Eng)

Digitized moment-arm reference data used to **validate** the ITB model's hip moment
arms. **The numeric validation** that consumes these bands (within-band % + RMSE/MAE
tables, model sweeps for figures) lives in the parent folder, **`../`**
(`validate_moment_arms.py`).
Source panels are the Blemker & Delp 3D-model gluteus-maximus moment-arm
figures (the cyan "3D Model Gluteus Maximus Fibers" band; black overlays are the
Nemeth/Ohlsen, Dostel, and Delp reference datasets). WebPlotDigitizer was used to
click the **upper and lower edges** of each fiber band, so every bound has a
different, irregular number of points.

## Processing

Per (coordinate, bound): fit a **4th-degree polynomial** (degree matched to Eng et
al.'s experimental fits) to the digitized points, then resample onto a uniform
**201-point** grid over Blemker's ROM. Fit residuals are sub-mm (max 0.057 cm), and
extrapolation is negligible (grids reach at most ~2° past the digitized data).

Long-table columns: `angle_type, moment_arm_type, angle_deg, moment_arm_cm,
bound_type, grid_idx` (`grid_idx` = 0–200). For Blemker `angle_type == moment_arm_type`
(each panel varies the same DOF whose MA it reports).

## Convention (verified against `Eng_2015_model_step7.osim`)

Our model's hip coords are **+flexion / +adduction / +internal-rotation** (Rajagopal
lineage; confirmed empirically — every glute-max tract computes as an extensor and
external rotator). The processed data is cast into this convention:

| raw panel | Blemker MA axis | → model coord | angle | moment arm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| extension | hip **extension** MA | `hip_flexion` | ×1 | **×−1** |
| adduction | hip **adduction** MA | `hip_adduction` | ×1 | ×1 |
| rotation | hip **internal-rotation** MA | `hip_rotation` | ×1 | ×1 |

Only the extension panel flips sign; the angle axes already match on all three.

## ⚠️ GOTCHA — what `lower`/`upper` means

`bound_type` labels **Blemker's original fiber-band edge** (the min/max moment arm
across fibers at each angle), **not** the visual top/bottom of the plotted curve.

Because the `hip_flexion` panel is sign-flipped (extension→flexion), its **`upper`
bound — the largest extension MA, i.e. the *most extensor* fibers — plots at the
BOTTOM** in model convention. `adduction` and `rotation` are not flipped, so their
`upper` stays on top. Don't assume `upper` is the visually-higher curve; it isn't for
flexion. (This applies to **Blemker only** — the Eng data below is never sign-flipped,
so its `upper` is always the visually-higher curve.)

# Eng et al. (2015) ITB tract moment-arm bands

Eng plots a separate uncertainty band per **individual tract**, each over its own
experimentally-sampled x-range. Our model collapses tract pairs into one tract, so
most panels carry **two partially-overlapping bands**:

| model tract | Eng sub-tracts (in the raw CSVs) |
|---|---|
| `glmax12_ITB` | `gmax1-itb`, `gmax2-itb` |
| `glmax34_ITB` | `gmax3-itb`, `gmax4-itb` |
| `tfl12_ITB` | `tfl1-itb`, `tfl2-itb` |

**Exception:** `tfl1-itb` never crossed the knee in Eng's measurement, so the
`tfl12` **knee-flexion** panel has only the `tfl2-itb` band (all other panels have two).

## Processing (`process_eng_moment_arms.py`)

Per (tract, bound): fit a **4th-degree polynomial**, then resample both bounds onto a
shared **201-point grid = the INTERSECTION of that tract's upper & lower x-ranges**.
Intersection (not union) means neither bound is ever extrapolated — the trimmed ends
are sub-0.1° digitization fuzz. Each tract keeps its own grid (bands have different
x-extents by design). All fit residuals are sub-mm (max 0.052 cm).

Raw header quirks were normalized at the source: `band_type`→`bound_type` (hip-flexion
files) and `knee_flexion_moment_cm`→`knee_flexion_moment_arm_cm` (knee files).

Long-table columns: `model_tract, muscle, angle_type, moment_arm_type, angle_deg,
moment_arm_cm, bound_type, grid_idx` — the Blemker core columns plus `model_tract`
(the combined model tract) and `muscle` (the Eng sub-tract).

## Convention — **no sign flips** (verified vs `Eng_2015_model_step7.osim`)

Eng's SIMM model is the ancestor of ours, so its data is already in our convention on
**all three axes**: hip_flexion (extensors read negative), hip_adduction (adductors
positive / abductors negative), and knee_flexion (`knee_angle_r` is [0,120]°,
flexion-positive). Every tract's model MA matches Eng's sign, so `angle ×1, moment_arm
×1` throughout — nothing is flipped (contrast Blemker's extension→flexion ×−1).

## Anomaly scan

`process_eng_moment_arms.py` flags residual-outlier points (|resid| > max(0.2 cm,
4·RMS)), band inversions (upper < lower), and degenerate (non-overlapping) grids. On
the current digitization it reports **none** — fits clean, no inversions, no outliers.

## Eng **SIMM-model** curves + the model-vs-data comparison

Alongside the cadaver **ranges** above, we also digitized what Eng's own **SIMM model**
reported for the same 3 tracts × 3 DOFs (hip flexion, hip adduction, knee flexion) — the
`eng-*-SIMM-raw.csv` files, one single curve each (no uncertainty band).
`process_eng_simm_moment_arms.py` fits each with the same **deg-4 poly**, resamples to a
201-pt grid over its digitized x-range (never extrapolated), and writes
`eng-simm-model-moment-arms-long.csv` (columns as the cadaver table but **no `bound_type`**,
since these are single lines) + a provenance JSON. Same convention — no sign flips. Fits are
clean (RMS ≤ 0.09 cm, no outliers).

The repo-root `make_figures.py` (`check_F04`) then makes the **3×3 comparison**
(`plots/check_F04.png`): each panel overlays the **Eng SIMM model** (black dotted), the
**Eng cadaver range** (grey shaded), and our grafted model (**step14**, red).
Reading: step2 tracks Eng's SIMM model closely in the smooth regions (as an exact port should) but
**inherits Eng's wrap glitches** — the step2 line spikes at the documented artifacts (glmax34_ITB
hip flexion ≈ 53°; both glmax knee panels ≈ 90–95°); step14 is smooth there (tori/knee-ellipsoids →
via-points, steps 3–6) and stays within/near the Eng model & cadaver data across the physiological ROM.
